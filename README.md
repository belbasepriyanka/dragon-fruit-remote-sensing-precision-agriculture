# Dragon Fruit Remote Sensing & Precision Agriculture

![Project preview](assets/preview.svg)

A research-oriented geospatial portfolio connecting **field measurements, plant and soil data, hyperspectral sensing, satellite/UAV observations, GIS, statistics, and machine learning** for vegetation monitoring and precision-agriculture applications.

## Project focus

This repository is designed to organize analytical workflows around:

- Plant and soil measurements
- Hyperspectral reflectance and spectral signatures
- Vegetation indices
- Nutrient-status analysis
- Satellite and UAV remote sensing
- Statistical analysis
- Machine-learning exploration
- Google Earth Engine processing

## Research context

The portfolio is connected to doctoral research on dragon fruit production, plant and soil nutrient status, vegetation stress, spectral reflectance, and environmental responses under different growing conditions.

Where demonstration or sample data are used, they should be treated as workflow examples rather than substitutes for measured research datasets.

## Applications

Precision agriculture, vegetation monitoring, nutrient-stress assessment, plant-health screening, multi-sensor Earth observation, and environmental data integration.
